Morning Routine
0 / 8 Done
Do this before 9:15 AM
These 5 steps take 10 minutes and protect you from bad trades. Non-negotiable daily habit before opening any chart.
GIFT Nifty Check (SGX)
Tells you which direction the Indian market will open — before 9:15 AM.
What is this?
GIFT Nifty is like a weather forecast for today market. If it is +100 points at 8 AM, NIFTY will open 100 points higher at 9:15 AM. If it is -200, India opens weak.
Open GIFT NiftyExplain this
Global Markets Check
How US markets closed last night and how Asian markets are trading now.
What is this?
If Dow Jones fell 2% last night, India will open weak. Asian markets open before India at 7:30 AM IST. All green = India opens positively.
Open Global MarketsExplain this
NSE Pre-Open Market
Between 9:00–9:15 AM orders are collected. Shows which stocks will gap up or down at open.
What is this?
NSE collects orders before 9:15 AM. Stocks with strong pre-open interest often continue momentum for the first 30–60 minutes after market opens.
Open NSE Pre-OpenExplain this
FII / DII Activity
FII = Foreign big money. DII = Indian big money (LIC, SBI MF). Their buying or selling moves the entire market.
What is this?
When FII buys Rs.2,000 crore of stocks, huge money enters and prices go up. Trade WITH big money. FII buying 3-4 days in a row = your buy trades have much higher success rate.
Open FII/DII DataExplain this
Set Your Trade Plan
Before 9:15 AM, decide which 2–3 stocks to watch. Write entry, stop loss, and target.
What is this?
A trade plan means you decide BEFORE market opens: stock, entry price, stop loss, and target. Without this before 9:15 AM, you will make emotional decisions. That almost always leads to losses.
